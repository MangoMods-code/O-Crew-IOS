The big one: O-Crew 2.0

What's new in this update
• Stores look like real stores now: tiled floors, shop windows with a street outside, front doors, a checkout counter, posters, ceiling lights and soft shadows
• Proper aisles down the middle of the store, with numbered end panels, instead of everything lined up on the walls
• New shelves, fridges and freezers, and items stack back into the shelf so a full spot looks fully stocked
• Hanging signs list every section on the unit, with a bar that fills up as you stock it and turns green when it's done
• Scoring and combos: chain correct placements for up to x5 points, plus bonuses for finishing a section, beating the clock and making no mistakes
• Locker: unlock new glove and basket colors with stars, and see your lifetime stats
• Background music, new combo and celebration sounds, with a separate music volume slider
• New main menu with a live store behind it, and an animated level complete screen
• New settings: edge smoothing, shadows, score display
• The game pauses by itself when you leave the app
• Levels you had saved mid-way start fresh once, because every store layout changed. Stars and best times are kept.
